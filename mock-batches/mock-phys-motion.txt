1. The SI unit of speed is
A: metre per second
B: kilometre per hour
C: metre per second squared
D: newton
Answer: A
Explanation: Speed is distance per time, m/s in SI units.

2. A car covers 100 m in 20 s. Its average speed is
A: 20 m/s
B: 5 m/s
C: 50 m/s
D: 0.2 m/s
Answer: B
Explanation: 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m/s.

3. The SI unit of acceleration is
A: m/s
B: km/h
C: m/s²
D: N/kg
Answer: C
Explanation: Acceleration is change of velocity per time.

4. A body moving with uniform velocity travels
A: with changing speed
B: along a circular path only
C: with increasing acceleration
D: equal distances in equal times in one direction
Answer: D
Explanation: Uniform velocity means constant speed in a straight line.

5. A body starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Its final velocity is
A: 10 m/s
B: 2.5 m/s
C: 7 m/s
D: 25 m/s
Answer: A
Explanation: v = u + at = 0 + 2×5 = 10 m/s.

6. A body starts from rest with acceleration 4 m/s². The distance covered in 3 s is
A: 12 m
B: 18 m
C: 24 m
D: 36 m
Answer: B
Explanation: s = ½at² = ½×4×9 = 18 m.

7. The acceleration of a body falling freely near the Earth is about
A: 1 m/s²
B: 100 m/s²
C: 10 m/s²
D: zero
Answer: C
Explanation: Free-fall acceleration g is about 9.8, roughly 10 m/s².

8. The slope of a distance-time graph gives
A: acceleration
B: force
C: distance
D: speed
Answer: D
Explanation: Slope of distance against time is speed.

9. The slope of a velocity-time graph gives
A: acceleration
B: distance
C: speed only
D: displacement only
Answer: A
Explanation: Change of velocity over time is acceleration.

10. The area under a velocity-time graph gives
A: the acceleration
B: the distance travelled
C: the force
D: the momentum
Answer: B
Explanation: Velocity × time summed gives distance.

11. At the highest point of its flight, a ball thrown vertically upwards has
A: zero acceleration
B: maximum velocity
C: zero velocity but non-zero acceleration
D: maximum kinetic energy
Answer: C
Explanation: Velocity momentarily vanishes while gravity still acts.

12. The momentum of a body is the product of its
A: mass and acceleration
B: weight and height
C: force and time squared
D: mass and velocity
Answer: D
Explanation: p = mv.

13. Newton's first law of motion is also called the law of
A: inertia
B: momentum
C: action and reaction
D: gravitation
Answer: A
Explanation: A body keeps its state unless forced: inertia.

14. The force needed to accelerate a 4 kg body at 3 m/s² is
A: 7 N
B: 12 N
C: 1.33 N
D: 36 N
Answer: B
Explanation: F = ma = 4×3 = 12 N.

15. A car moving at constant velocity has a resultant force of
A: mg
B: ma
C: zero
D: its weight
Answer: C
Explanation: Constant velocity means no acceleration, hence no net force.

16. From 20 m/s to rest in 5 s, a vehicle's deceleration is
A: 5 m/s²
B: 100 m/s²
C: 0.25 m/s²
D: 4 m/s²
Answer: D
Explanation: 20/5 = 4 m/s².

17. A speed of 72 km/h expressed in m/s is
A: 20 m/s
B: 7.2 m/s
C: 200 m/s
D: 36 m/s
Answer: A
Explanation: 72 × 1000/3600 = 20 m/s.

18. A cyclist rides at 10 m/s for 30 s. The distance covered is
A: 3 m
B: 300 m
C: 30 m
D: 600 m
Answer: B
Explanation: 10 × 30 = 300 m.

19. A body thrown horizontally from a height follows a
A: straight horizontal line
B: vertical line
C: parabolic path
D: circular path
Answer: C
Explanation: Horizontal motion plus free fall gives a parabola.

20. The weight of a 5 kg body where g = 10 m/s² is
A: 5 N
B: 25 N
C: 500 N
D: 50 N
Answer: D
Explanation: W = mg = 5×10 = 50 N.

21. A satellite moves in a circle at constant speed. Its velocity is
A: changing because its direction changes
B: constant in every way
C: zero
D: increasing steadily
Answer: A
Explanation: Direction keeps changing, so the vector velocity changes.

22. Newton's third law states that
A: force equals mass times acceleration
B: action and reaction are equal and opposite
C: every body resists change
D: energy is conserved
Answer: B
Explanation: Forces act in equal and opposite pairs.

23. A bus starts from rest and reaches 24 m/s in 8 s. Its acceleration is
A: 8 m/s²
B: 32 m/s²
C: 3 m/s²
D: 0.33 m/s²
Answer: C
Explanation: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 m/s².

24. The average speed of a journey is
A: the mean of all speeds
B: half the maximum speed
C: total time over total distance
D: total distance divided by total time
Answer: D
Explanation: Average speed = total distance/total time.

25. Which graph has time on the horizontal axis and distance on the vertical axis?
A: a distance-time graph
B: a velocity-time graph
C: a force-extension graph
D: a pressure-volume graph
Answer: A
Explanation: Distance-time plots distance against time.
