1. According to the kinetic theory of matter, the particles of matter
A: are in constant random motion
B: are always at rest
C: all carry the same charge
D: repel each other only
Answer: A
Explanation: Kinetic theory holds that particles move constantly and randomly.

2. The change of state from solid directly to gas is called
A: condensation
B: sublimation
C: freezing
D: melting
Answer: B
Explanation: Sublimation skips the liquid state.

3. Diffusion occurs fastest in
A: solids
B: liquids
C: gases
D: colloids
Answer: C
Explanation: Gas particles are freest and furthest apart, so they diffuse fastest.

4. The irregular zig-zag movement of smoke particles in air is called
A: diffusion
B: osmosis
C: sublimation
D: Brownian motion
Answer: D
Explanation: Brownian motion is the random motion of particles bombarded by molecules.

5. Boiling differs from evaporation in that boiling occurs
A: throughout the liquid at a fixed temperature
B: only at the surface at any temperature
C: only in the presence of wind
D: without heat
Answer: A
Explanation: Boiling is bulk vaporisation at a fixed boiling point.

6. Under standard pressure, pure ice melts at
A: 100 °C
B: 0 °C
C: 32 °C
D: -10 °C
Answer: B
Explanation: The melting point of pure ice at 1 atm is 0 °C.

7. Gases are easily compressed because their particles
A: are very heavy
B: repel each other
C: are far apart
D: carry no energy
Answer: C
Explanation: Large spaces between gas particles allow compression.

8. When a metal rod is heated, it expands because its particles
A: increase in size
B: multiply in number
C: lose energy
D: move faster and vibrate over larger amplitudes
Answer: D
Explanation: Heating raises particle motion, increasing average spacing.

9. The pressure exerted by a gas in a container is due to
A: collisions of its particles with the walls
B: the weight of the container
C: gravity on the lid
D: friction between particles
Answer: A
Explanation: Gas pressure arises from molecular bombardment of the walls.

10. Evaporation of a liquid causes cooling because
A: the container absorbs heat
B: the fastest particles escape, taking energy away
C: particles slow down on leaving
D: air presses on the liquid
Answer: B
Explanation: The most energetic molecules leave, lowering the average energy left behind.

11. Solids have fixed shapes because their particles
A: move at high speed
B: are far apart
C: are tightly held in fixed positions
D: carry opposite charges
Answer: C
Explanation: Fixed lattice positions give solids their shape.

12. The spreading of bromine vapour in a closed tube of air demonstrates
A: the weight of gases
B: chemical combination
C: the effect of gravity on liquids
D: the movement of particles
Answer: D
Explanation: The spread shows particles of both gases moving and mixing.

13. Which of the following is a physical change?
A: the melting of ice
B: the rusting of iron
C: the burning of paper
D: the souring of milk
Answer: A
Explanation: Melting changes state only; no new substance forms.

14. The change of state from gas to liquid is called
A: evaporation
B: condensation
C: sublimation
D: solidification
Answer: B
Explanation: Condensation is gas turning to liquid.

15. When the temperature of a gas falls, its particles
A: move faster
B: increase in size
C: slow down
D: stop completely
Answer: C
Explanation: Lower temperature means lower average kinetic energy.

16. A liquid has
A: fixed shape and fixed volume
B: no fixed volume and no fixed shape
C: fixed shape but no fixed volume
D: fixed volume but no fixed shape
Answer: D
Explanation: Liquids take the container's shape but keep their volume.

17. An everyday substance that sublimes is
A: naphthalene
B: common salt
C: water
D: ethanol
Answer: A
Explanation: Naphthalene (mothball) passes from solid to vapour.

18. A gas with a smaller molecular mass diffuses
A: slower than a heavier gas
B: faster than a heavier gas at the same temperature
C: at the same rate as a heavier gas
D: only in darkness
Answer: B
Explanation: Lighter particles move faster at the same temperature.

19. During melting, heat energy is
A: released
B: unchanged
C: absorbed
D: destroyed
Answer: C
Explanation: Melting absorbs latent heat of fusion.

20. In the solid state, particles
A: move about freely
B: rotate only in circles
C: are at absolute rest
D: vibrate about fixed positions
Answer: D
Explanation: Solid particles vibrate without leaving their lattice sites.

21. Perfume sprayed in one corner of a room soon reaches the other corner by
A: diffusion
B: osmosis
C: evaporation of the room
D: convection of cloth
Answer: A
Explanation: Gas molecules spread from high to low concentration by diffusion.

22. A gas can be liquefied by
A: lowering pressure and raising temperature
B: increasing pressure and lowering temperature
C: adding a catalyst
D: passing a current through it
Answer: B
Explanation: Compression plus cooling brings particles close enough to liquefy.

23. The change of state from liquid to solid is called
A: melting
B: condensation
C: freezing
D: boiling
Answer: C
Explanation: Freezing (solidification) turns liquid to solid.

24. As the temperature of a substance rises, the kinetic energy of its particles
A: decreases
B: remains constant
C: becomes negative
D: increases
Answer: D
Explanation: Temperature is a measure of average kinetic energy.

25. The state of matter with the strongest forces of attraction between particles is the
A: solid state
B: liquid state
C: gaseous state
D: plasma state
Answer: A
Explanation: Solids hold particles most tightly.
