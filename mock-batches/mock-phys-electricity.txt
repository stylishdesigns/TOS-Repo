1. A p.d. of 12 V drives a current through a 4 Ω resistor. The current is
A: 3 A
B: 48 A
C: 8 A
D: 0.33 A
Answer: A
Explanation: I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A.

2. The SI unit of resistance is the
A: ampere
B: ohm
C: volt
D: watt
Answer: B
Explanation: Resistance is measured in ohms.

3. The SI unit of electric current is the
A: volt
B: ohm
C: ampere
D: coulomb
Answer: C
Explanation: Current is measured in amperes.

4. Ohm's law states that
A: voltage varies inversely with current
B: resistance varies with current
C: power varies with charge
D: current varies directly with voltage at constant temperature
Answer: D
Explanation: V = IR at constant temperature.

5. The total resistance of 2 Ω, 3 Ω and 5 Ω resistors connected in series is
A: 10 Ω
B: 0.6 Ω
C: 1.0 Ω
D: 30 Ω
Answer: A
Explanation: Series resistances add: 2+3+5 = 10 Ω.

6. Two 6 Ω resistors connected in parallel give a combined resistance of
A: 12 Ω
B: 3 Ω
C: 6 Ω
D: 0.33 Ω
Answer: B
Explanation: Equal parallel resistors halve: 6/2 = 3 Ω.

7. The power dissipated by a device drawing 2 A at 12 V is
A: 6 W
B: 14 W
C: 24 W
D: 144 W
Answer: C
Explanation: P = VI = 12×2 = 24 W.

8. The electrical power dissipated in a resistor equals
A: IR
B: V/R
C: I/V
D: I²R
Answer: D
Explanation: P = I²R = V²/R.

9. The main purpose of a fuse in a circuit is to
A: protect the circuit from excessive current
B: increase the current
C: store charge
D: reduce the voltage
Answer: A
Explanation: The fuse melts when current exceeds its rating.

10. In a correct circuit connection, an ammeter is connected in
A: parallel and a voltmeter in series
B: series and a voltmeter in parallel
C: series with a voltmeter also in series
D: parallel with a voltmeter also in parallel
Answer: B
Explanation: Ammeters carry the current; voltmeters bridge the component.

11. In the external circuit, conventional current flows from the
A: negative terminal to the positive
B: earth to the sky
C: positive terminal to the negative terminal
D: load to the source
Answer: C
Explanation: Conventional current leaves the positive terminal.

12. The charge carried when a steady 2 A flows for 60 s is
A: 30 C
B: 62 C
C: 7200 C
D: 120 C
Answer: D
Explanation: Q = It = 2×60 = 120 C.

13. For a metallic conductor, resistance normally
A: increases as temperature rises
B: falls as temperature rises
C: is independent of temperature
D: falls as length increases
Answer: A
Explanation: Hotter lattices scatter electrons more.

14. A 60 W lamp burning for 10 hours consumes
A: 6 kWh
B: 0.6 kWh
C: 60 kWh
D: 600 kWh
Answer: B
Explanation: 60 W × 10 h = 600 Wh = 0.6 kWh.

15. Which of these is a good conductor of electricity?
A: rubber
B: glass
C: copper
D: plastic
Answer: C
Explanation: Copper is a standard conductor.

16. An example of an insulator is
A: iron
B: sea water
C: graphite
D: rubber
Answer: D
Explanation: Rubber does not conduct.

17. In a series circuit, the current at every point is
A: the same
B: different at each point
C: zero
D: equal to the voltage
Answer: A
Explanation: Series circuits carry one current.

18. In a parallel circuit, the voltage across each branch is
A: different on each branch
B: the same
C: zero
D: half the total
Answer: B
Explanation: Branches share the same two nodes.

19. Adding more resistors in parallel
A: increases the total resistance
B: does not change resistance
C: decreases the total resistance
D: cuts the current to zero
Answer: C
Explanation: More parallel paths ease the flow.

20. The electromotive force of a cell is
A: the current it delivers
B: the heat it produces
C: its internal resistance
D: the work done per coulomb by the cell in driving charge round the circuit
Answer: D
Explanation: Emf is energy supplied per unit charge.

21. A 9 V battery is connected to a 3 Ω resistor. The current is
A: 3 A
B: 27 A
C: 0.33 A
D: 6 A
Answer: A
Explanation: I = 9/3 = 3 A.

22. The unit of electrical power is the
A: ohm
B: watt
C: coulomb
D: joule per coulomb
Answer: B
Explanation: One watt is one joule per second.

23. A 100 W bulb used for 5 hours costs, at 10 naira per kWh,
A: 0.5 naira
B: 50 naira
C: 5 naira
D: 500 naira
Answer: C
Explanation: 0.5 kWh × 10 = 5 naira.

24. A thicker wire of the same material and length has
A: more resistance
B: the same resistance
C: infinite resistance
D: less resistance
Answer: D
Explanation: A larger cross-section lets charge flow more easily.

25. A short circuit is a path of
A: very low resistance which draws a dangerously large current
B: high resistance which blocks current
C: zero voltage
D: no connection at all
Answer: A
Explanation: Low-resistance bypasses cause current surges.
