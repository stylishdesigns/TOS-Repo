1. In a food chain, the organisms that manufacture their own food are the
A: producers
B: consumers
C: decomposers
D: parasites
Answer: A
Explanation: Green plants produce food by photosynthesis.

2. An organism that feeds directly on producers is a
A: producer
B: primary consumer
C: tertiary consumer
D: decomposer
Answer: B
Explanation: Herbivores that eat plants are primary consumers.

3. The arrows in a food chain show the direction of
A: blood flow
B: water absorption
C: energy flow
D: age increase
Answer: C
Explanation: Arrows point from food to the eater: energy flow.

4. Bacteria and fungi that break down dead matter are
A: producers
B: herbivores
C: predators
D: decomposers
Answer: D
Explanation: Decomposers recycle nutrients.

5. The natural place where an organism lives is its
A: habitat
B: niche
C: biome
D: range
Answer: A
Explanation: Habitat is the address of the organism.

6. All the individuals of one species living in an area at a given time form a
A: community
B: population
C: ecosystem
D: biosphere
Answer: B
Explanation: One species, one area: population.

7. All the populations of different species living together in an area form a
A: population
B: family
C: community
D: niche
Answer: C
Explanation: Many species together: community.

8. A community together with its physical environment forms an
A: organ system
B: habitat
C: ecotype
D: ecosystem
Answer: D
Explanation: That definition is an ecosystem.

9. As energy passes along a food chain, the amount available at each level
A: decreases
B: increases
C: stays constant
D: doubles
Answer: A
Explanation: Energy is lost as heat at each transfer.

10. An animal that feeds only on plants is a
A: carnivore
B: herbivore
C: omnivore
D: saprophyte
Answer: B
Explanation: Goats and rabbits are herbivores.

11. A lion is best described as a
A: herbivore
B: producer
C: carnivore
D: decomposer
Answer: C
Explanation: Lions eat flesh: carnivores.

12. An organism that eats both plants and animals is an
A: herbivore
B: carnivore
C: autotroph
D: omnivore
Answer: D
Explanation: Man and pigs are omnivores.

13. A pond is an example of a
A: habitat
B: tissue
C: niche
D: population
Answer: A
Explanation: A pond is a place where organisms live.

14. The specific role an organism plays in its habitat is its
A: habitat
B: niche
C: biome
D: shelter
Answer: B
Explanation: Niche is the organism's job, habitat its address.

15. In a pyramid of numbers for a grassland, the level with the largest number of organisms is usually the
A: primary consumers
B: secondary consumers
C: producers
D: tertiary consumers
Answer: C
Explanation: Many grass plants support fewer insects and still fewer hawks.

16. In the nitrogen cycle, the bacteria that convert atmospheric nitrogen into nitrates carry out
A: denitrification
B: photosynthesis
C: transpiration
D: nitrogen fixation
Answer: D
Explanation: Fixing bacteria turn N2 into usable nitrates.

17. Photosynthesis removes carbon dioxide from the air while
A: respiration adds it
B: photosynthesis also adds it
C: evaporation removes it
D: combustion removes it
Answer: A
Explanation: Respiration and combustion return CO2; photosynthesis takes it up.

18. A cactus with a thick water-storing stem living in the desert shows
A: mutation
B: adaptation
C: succession
D: competition between species
Answer: B
Explanation: Such features are adaptations to drought.

19. A relationship where both partners benefit is
A: parasitism
B: commensalism
C: mutualism
D: predation
Answer: C
Explanation: Both benefiting is mutualism.

20. A tapeworm living in the human gut, harming the host, shows
A: mutualism
B: saprophytism
C: symbiosis of equals
D: parasitism
Answer: D
Explanation: One benefits, the other is harmed: parasitism.

21. The gradual change in the species making up a community over time is
A: succession
B: evolution
C: pollution
D: migration
Answer: A
Explanation: That orderly change is succession.

22. Light, temperature and soil are examples of
A: biotic factors
B: abiotic factors
C: producers
D: decomposers
Answer: B
Explanation: Non-living factors are abiotic.

23. The maximum population of a species an environment can support is its
A: biomass
B: natality
C: carrying capacity
D: habitat size
Answer: C
Explanation: That ceiling is the carrying capacity.

24. A complex network of many interlinked food chains is a
A: food chain
B: pyramid of energy
C: nutrient cycle
D: food web
Answer: D
Explanation: Interlinked chains form a food web.

25. In the chain grass, grasshopper, lizard, hawk, the lizard is a
A: secondary consumer
B: producer
C: primary consumer
D: decomposer
Answer: A
Explanation: The lizard eats the primary consumer, so it is secondary.
