1. Find the mean of 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10.
A: 6
B: 5
C: 7
D: 8
Answer: A
Explanation: The sum 30 divided by 5 is 6.

2. Find the median of 3, 7, 9, 11 and 15.
A: 7
B: 9
C: 10
D: 11
Answer: B
Explanation: The middle value of the ordered list is 9.

3. Find the mode of 2, 3, 3, 5, 7, 3 and 8.
A: 2
B: 5
C: 3
D: 7
Answer: C
Explanation: 3 appears three times, more than any other value.

4. Find the range of 14, 9, 22 and 5.
A: 9
B: 14
C: 22
D: 17
Answer: D
Explanation: 22 - 5 = 17.

5. Five scores have a sum of 60. Their mean is
A: 12
B: 10
C: 15
D: 16
Answer: A
Explanation: 60 ÷ 5 = 12.

6. Find the median of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7.
A: 3.5
B: 4
C: 4.5
D: 5
Answer: B
Explanation: The fourth of seven ordered values is 4.

7. If every score in a set is increased by 5, the mean
A: is unchanged
B: increases by 1
C: increases by 5
D: doubles
Answer: C
Explanation: Adding a constant to every value adds that constant to the mean.

8. When no value in a data set repeats, the set has
A: mode 0
B: two modes
C: one mode
D: no mode
Answer: D
Explanation: A mode needs a repeated value.

9. Find the mean of 10, 20 and 30.
A: 20
B: 15
C: 25
D: 30
Answer: A
Explanation: 60 ÷ 3 = 20.

10. Find the median of 4, 8, 15, 16, 23 and 42.
A: 15
B: 15.5
C: 16
D: 19
Answer: B
Explanation: The two middle values are 15 and 16, so the median is 15.5.

11. Find the range of 100, 37, 68 and 45.
A: 55
B: 68
C: 63
D: 100
Answer: C
Explanation: 100 - 37 = 63.

12. Find the mean of the first five odd numbers.
A: 4
B: 6
C: 9
D: 5
Answer: D
Explanation: 1+3+5+7+9 = 25, and 25 ÷ 5 = 5.

13. A pie chart sector measures 90 degrees. As a percentage it represents
A: 25%
B: 20%
C: 30%
D: 40%
Answer: A
Explanation: 90/360 = 1/4 = 25%.

14. Find the median of 2, 4, 6 and 8.
A: 4
B: 5
C: 6
D: 6.5
Answer: B
Explanation: (4+6)/2 = 5.

15. The values 5, 5, 6 and 6 have as their modes
A: 5 only
B: 6 only
C: 5 and 6
D: no value
Answer: C
Explanation: Both 5 and 6 appear twice, so the data is bimodal.

16. The sum of the deviations of values from their mean is always
A: one
B: the mean
C: the range
D: zero
Answer: D
Explanation: Deviations above and below the mean cancel exactly.

17. The mean weight of five boys is 60 kg. If a 55 kg boy is replaced by a 65 kg boy, the new mean is
A: 62 kg
B: 60 kg
C: 61 kg
D: 65 kg
Answer: A
Explanation: Total rises by 10, so the mean rises by 10/5 = 2 to 62.

18. The median is preferred to the mean when a data set
A: is very large
B: contains an extreme value
C: has no mode
D: has equal values
Answer: B
Explanation: An outlier drags the mean but leaves the median steady.

19. In a frequency table, a score of 4 appears 6 times and a score of 5 appears 4 times. The total frequency is
A: 6
B: 9
C: 10
D: 20
Answer: C
Explanation: 6 + 4 = 10.

20. Find the mean of the counting numbers 1 to 10.
A: 5
B: 6
C: 11
D: 5.5
Answer: D
Explanation: 55 ÷ 10 = 5.5.

21. In 11 ordered values, the lower quartile is closest to the
A: third value
B: first value
C: fifth value
D: ninth value
Answer: A
Explanation: (11+1)/4 = 3, so the 3rd value.

22. The class interval 10 to 19 has a class midpoint of
A: 14
B: 14.5
C: 15
D: 29
Answer: B
Explanation: (10+19)/2 = 14.5.

23. A bar chart shows Red 3, Blue 5 and Green 2. The most common colour is
A: Red
B: Green
C: Blue
D: none
Answer: C
Explanation: Blue has the tallest bar.

24. The average of a, b and c is 8. Their sum is
A: 8
B: 16
C: 32
D: 24
Answer: D
Explanation: 3 × 8 = 24.

25. The variance of 2, 4, 6 and 8 is
A: 5
B: 4
C: 6
D: 25
Answer: A
Explanation: Mean 5; squared deviations 9,1,1,9 average to 5.
