1. The cell theory states that all living things are made up of
A: cells
B: tissues
C: organs
D: systems
Answer: A
Explanation: Cells are the basic units of life.

2. The organelle known as the powerhouse of the cell is the
A: ribosome
B: mitochondrion
C: lysosome
D: vacuole
Answer: B
Explanation: Mitochondria release energy in respiration.

3. Photosynthesis takes place in the
A: mitochondrion
B: nucleus
C: chloroplast
D: ribosome
Answer: C
Explanation: Chloroplasts contain the chlorophyll that traps light.

4. The part of the cell that controls all its activities is the
A: cell wall
B: vacuole
C: cytoplasm
D: nucleus
Answer: D
Explanation: The nucleus houses the genetic instructions.

5. A structure present in plant cells but absent in animal cells is the
A: cell wall
B: cell membrane
C: nucleus
D: mitochondrion
Answer: A
Explanation: Only plant cells have a cellulose cell wall.

6. Protein synthesis in the cell takes place on the
A: lysosomes
B: ribosomes
C: vacuoles
D: chloroplasts
Answer: B
Explanation: Ribosomes assemble amino acids into proteins.

7. The cell membrane controls
A: cell division only
B: the colour of the cell
C: what enters and leaves the cell
D: the shape of chromosomes
Answer: C
Explanation: The membrane is selectively permeable.

8. The largest organelle in a mature plant cell is the
A: nucleus
B: ribosome
C: chloroplast
D: central vacuole
Answer: D
Explanation: The sap-filled vacuole dominates the plant cell.

9. Ribosomes are found in
A: both plant and animal cells
B: plant cells only
C: animal cells only
D: bacteria only
Answer: A
Explanation: All cells need proteins, so all have ribosomes.

10. The movement of water from a dilute to a more concentrated solution through a semi-permeable membrane is
A: diffusion
B: osmosis
C: transpiration
D: active transport
Answer: B
Explanation: That definition is osmosis.

11. The movement of molecules from a region of higher concentration to one of lower concentration is
A: osmosis
B: phagocytosis
C: diffusion
D: pinocytosis
Answer: C
Explanation: That definition is diffusion.

12. A plant cell placed in a concentrated sugar solution loses water and its cytoplasm shrinks from the wall. This is
A: turgidity
B: haemolysis
C: ciliation
D: plasmolysis
Answer: D
Explanation: Water loss shrinks the protoplast: plasmolysis.

13. The correct order of levels of organisation is
A: cell, tissue, organ, system
B: tissue, cell, system, organ
C: organ, cell, tissue, system
D: system, organ, tissue, cell
Answer: A
Explanation: Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, organs form systems.

14. Which of these organisms is unicellular?
A: Mucor
B: Amoeba
C: Hydra
D: earthworm
Answer: B
Explanation: Amoeba is a single-celled organism.

15. Chromosomes carry the units of inheritance called
A: vacuoles
B: ribosomes
C: genes
D: plastids
Answer: C
Explanation: Genes are borne on chromosomes.

16. The organelle sometimes called the suicide bag because it digests worn-out cell parts is the
A: nucleolus
B: ribosome
C: chloroplast
D: lysosome
Answer: D
Explanation: Lysosomes hold digestive enzymes.

17. Prokaryotic cells, such as bacteria, differ from eukaryotic cells because they lack a
A: true nucleus
B: cell membrane
C: cytoplasm
D: ribosome
Answer: A
Explanation: Bacterial DNA is not enclosed in a nuclear membrane.

18. The ability of a microscope to show fine detail clearly is its
A: magnification
B: resolving power
C: field of view
D: brightness
Answer: B
Explanation: Resolution is the distinguishing of detail.

19. In green plants, light energy is trapped by
A: haemoglobin
B: melanin
C: chlorophyll
D: carotene in roots
Answer: C
Explanation: Chlorophyll absorbs light for photosynthesis.

20. Movement of substances against a concentration gradient, requiring energy, is
A: diffusion
B: osmosis
C: evaporation
D: active transport
Answer: D
Explanation: Active transport uses energy to pump against the gradient.

21. Chloroplasts are found
A: only in plant cells
B: only in animal cells
C: in all cells
D: only in bacteria
Answer: A
Explanation: Chloroplasts belong to photosynthetic plant cells.

22. The jelly-like medium in which cell organelles are suspended is the
A: nucleoplasm
B: cytoplasm
C: cell sap
D: lymph
Answer: B
Explanation: Cytoplasm hosts the organelles and many reactions.

23. In mammals, mature red blood cells are unusual because they
A: have two nuclei
B: are green in colour
C: have no nucleus
D: carry no haemoglobin
Answer: C
Explanation: Mammalian red cells expel their nucleus.

24. A group of similar cells performing the same function is a
A: system
B: organ
C: organism
D: tissue
Answer: D
Explanation: That definition is a tissue.

25. Cell sap is mainly found in the
A: vacuole
B: nucleus
C: ribosome
D: cell wall
Answer: A
Explanation: The vacuole stores cell sap.
