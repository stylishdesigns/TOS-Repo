1. The element with atomic number 11 is
A: sodium
B: magnesium
C: neon
D: potassium
Answer: A
Explanation: Atomic number 11 is sodium (Na).

2. The elements of Group 1 are collectively called
A: halogens
B: alkali metals
C: noble gases
D: transition metals
Answer: B
Explanation: Li, Na, K and the rest are the alkali metals.

3. The elements of Group 17 are called
A: alkali metals
B: alkaline earth metals
C: halogens
D: actinides
Answer: C
Explanation: Fluorine to astatine form the halogen family.

4. Noble gases are chemically unreactive because their outer electron shells are
A: only half filled
B: completely empty
C: unstable
D: completely filled
Answer: D
Explanation: Full outer shells leave no tendency to gain or lose electrons.

5. In the modern periodic table, the horizontal rows are called
A: periods
B: groups
C: families
D: blocks
Answer: A
Explanation: Rows are periods; columns are groups.

6. The modern periodic table arranges elements in order of increasing
A: atomic mass
B: atomic number
C: density
D: reactivity
Answer: B
Explanation: Moseley's ordering is by atomic (proton) number.

7. Across a period from left to right, the atomic radius
A: increases
B: remains constant
C: decreases
D: doubles
Answer: C
Explanation: Rising nuclear charge pulls the same shell closer.

8. The most reactive metals are found in
A: Group 18
B: Group 17
C: Group 7
D: Group 1
Answer: D
Explanation: Alkali metals lose their single outer electron most readily.

9. The halogen commonly used to disinfect water is
A: chlorine
B: iodine
C: bromine
D: helium
Answer: A
Explanation: Chlorine kills waterborne germs.

10. An atom of chlorine (atomic number 17) has how many electrons in its outermost shell?
A: 5
B: 7
C: 6
D: 8
Answer: B
Explanation: Chlorine is 2,8,7, so seven outer electrons.

11. Transition elements are noted for forming
A: colourless ions with one fixed state
B: only gaseous compounds
C: coloured compounds and variable oxidation states
D: no compounds at all
Answer: C
Explanation: Their partly filled d orbitals give colour and variable valency.

12. The elements of Group 2 are called
A: halogens
B: coinage metals
C: lanthanides
D: alkaline earth metals
Answer: D
Explanation: Be, Mg, Ca and the rest form Group 2.

13. The symbol Fe represents
A: iron
B: fluorine
C: francium
D: fermium
Answer: A
Explanation: Fe is iron, a transition metal.

14. Down Group 1, the reactivity of the metals
A: decreases
B: increases
C: stays constant
D: falls to zero
Answer: B
Explanation: Outer electrons are lost more easily as the atoms grow larger.

15. Down Group 17, the reactivity of the halogens
A: increases
B: stays constant
C: decreases
D: becomes metallic
Answer: C
Explanation: Larger atoms attract the extra electron less strongly.

16. Isotopes of an element have the same
A: mass number but different atomic numbers
B: number of neutrons
C: chemical symbol only
D: atomic number but different mass numbers
Answer: D
Explanation: Isotopes differ only in neutron count.

17. The noble gas used to fill balloons because it is light and unreactive is
A: helium
B: neon
C: argon
D: xenon
Answer: A
Explanation: Helium is both lighter than air and inert.

18. Silicon is best classified as a
A: alkali metal
B: metalloid
C: halogen
D: noble gas
Answer: B
Explanation: Silicon shows both metallic and non-metallic character.

19. Elements of Group 16 have how many electrons in their outermost shell?
A: 2
B: 5
C: 6
D: 7
Answer: C
Explanation: Group 16 (chalcogens) carry six outer electrons.

20. The element with atomic number 20 belongs to
A: Group 1
B: Group 17
C: Group 18
D: Group 2
Answer: D
Explanation: Calcium (2,8,8,2) sits in Group 2.

21. Oxides of metals are usually
A: basic
B: acidic
C: neutral gases
D: amphoteric always
Answer: A
Explanation: Metal oxides react with acids as bases.

22. The most abundant element in the universe is
A: oxygen
B: hydrogen
C: helium
D: carbon
Answer: B
Explanation: Hydrogen makes up about three quarters of ordinary matter.

23. Alkali metals are stored under oil to prevent them
A: melting
B: evaporating
C: reacting with air and moisture
D: becoming magnetic
Answer: C
Explanation: They react vigorously with oxygen and water.

24. Which of these is a halogen?
A: sodium
B: calcium
C: argon
D: fluorine
Answer: D
Explanation: Fluorine heads Group 17.

25. The vertical columns of the periodic table are called
A: groups
B: periods
C: series only
D: orbitals
Answer: A
Explanation: Columns are groups (families).
