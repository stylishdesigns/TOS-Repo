1. The old bridge groaned under the weight of the lorry. The word "groaned" suggests that the bridge was
A: under strain
B: musical
C: newly built
D: abandoned
Answer: A
Explanation: Groaning under weight points to strain, not to music, age or vacancy.

2. Musa counted his money twice before paying, then counted it a third time. This suggests Musa was
A: generous
B: careful with money
C: very wealthy
D: careless
Answer: B
Explanation: Counting three times shows care, not wealth or carelessness.

3. The news spread like harmattan fire. The expression means the news spread
A: slowly
B: quietly
C: very quickly
D: only in the dry season
Answer: C
Explanation: Harmattan fire is a comparison for speed of spreading.

4. Ada's voice was calm, but her hands shook as she signed the form. This suggests Ada was
A: confident and relaxed
B: angry with the clerk
C: too weak to write
D: hiding her nervousness
Answer: D
Explanation: The calm voice with shaking hands shows concealed nerves.

5. The teacher's remark was a slap in the face. The remark was
A: insulting
B: musical
C: affectionate
D: a punishment ordered by the school
Answer: A
Explanation: A slap in the face is a metaphor for an insulting remark.

6. Bola, who rarely visits, arrived with a suitcase. This suggests Bola intended to
A: return the suitcase to its owner
B: stay for some time
C: catch a flight immediately
D: sell the suitcase
Answer: B
Explanation: A suitcase from a rare visitor signals an extended stay.

7. The committee's report was thin on facts but thick on excuses. The report mostly contained
A: measured data
B: clear recommendations
C: justifications
D: helpful diagrams
Answer: C
Explanation: Thick on excuses means full of justifications, thin on facts.

8. Even the pigeons left the square when the soldiers marched in. The sentence stresses
A: the pigeons' love of noise
B: the hunger of the pigeons
C: the emptiness of the square before
D: the fear in the scene
Answer: D
Explanation: Even the birds fleeing stresses how frightening the arrival was.

9. "If you finish early, you may go." This means the listener
A: is permitted to go
B: must finish early
C: is forced to go
D: cannot go at all
Answer: A
Explanation: May expresses permission.

10. Chike had a word with the driver. This means Chike
A: taught the driver a new word
B: spoke briefly with the driver
C: quarrelled loudly with the driver
D: wrote a letter to the driver
Answer: B
Explanation: Have a word with means a short conversation.

11. The examinations are just round the corner. This means they are
A: far away
B: cancelled
C: near
D: held outdoors
Answer: C
Explanation: Round the corner means close at hand.

12. Ngozi took the blame although the fault was shared. Ngozi can be described as
A: greedy
B: forgetful
C: easily frightened
D: ready to accept responsibility
Answer: D
Explanation: Taking blame for a shared fault shows willingness to shoulder it.

13. The medicine smelt strongly of camphor. The sense appealed to is
A: smell
B: touch
C: taste
D: sight
Answer: A
Explanation: Smelt appeals to the sense of smell.

14. Half the class failed, not because the paper was hard but because nobody read. The failure was caused by
A: a hard paper
B: lack of preparation
C: a harsh teacher
D: a noisy hall
Answer: B
Explanation: The passage names lack of reading, not the paper or the hall.

15. The boy's results were the talk of the town. The results were
A: kept secret
B: very poor
C: widely discussed
D: known to be forged
Answer: C
Explanation: Talk of the town means on everyone's lips.

16. Father growled at us, but his eyes twinkled. This suggests Father was
A: very angry indeed
B: afraid of the children
C: indifferent to us
D: pretending to be angry
Answer: D
Explanation: Twinkling eyes with a growl show play-acted anger.

17. The candidate spoke off the cuff. This means he spoke
A: without preparation
B: from a written script
C: in a whisper
D: with a cold
Answer: A
Explanation: Off the cuff means impromptu.

18. "Where there is a will, there is a way" teaches that
A: every road has a signpost
B: determination finds a means
C: wishes are dangerous
D: wealth opens all doors
Answer: B
Explanation: The proverb prizes determination over difficulty.

19. The manager gave us a thousand and one reasons for the delay. The figure of speech is
A: simile
B: irony
C: hyperbole
D: personification
Answer: C
Explanation: A thousand and one is deliberate exaggeration.

20. Though the road was busy, Tunde walked as if he had all the time in the world. Tunde walked
A: in great haste
B: like a racer
C: with heavy luggage
D: unhurriedly
Answer: D
Explanation: Having all the time in the world suggests an unhurried pace.

21. The beggar's plea fell on deaf ears. This means the plea was
A: ignored
B: answered at once
C: spoken too softly to hear
D: made to deaf people only
Answer: A
Explanation: Fall on deaf ears means to be ignored.

22. She nursed the wounded bird back to health. Nursed here means she
A: fed it to the cat
B: cared for it until it recovered
C: caged it for ever
D: sold it in the market
Answer: B
Explanation: Nurse back to health means care until recovery.

23. The principal's speech drew a long applause. The audience's reaction shows the speech was
A: poorly delivered
B: cut short
C: well received
D: unheard
Answer: C
Explanation: Long applause signals a warm reception.

24. Life in the village was anything but dull. This means village life was
A: boring
B: silent
C: hardly existing
D: interesting
Answer: D
Explanation: Anything but dull means the opposite of dull.

25. When the result came out, Kunle was beside himself with joy. Kunle was
A: overjoyed
B: calm and quiet
C: full of sorrow
D: standing outside himself
Answer: A
Explanation: Beside himself with joy means overwhelmed with joy.

26. The flood left the village in its wake. In its wake here means
A: ahead of it
B: behind it
C: inside it
D: against it
Answer: B
Explanation: In its wake means in the trail left behind.

27. Rather than help, his advice only added fuel to the fire. His advice
A: solved the problem
B: cooled tempers
C: made the situation worse
D: was ignored
Answer: C
Explanation: Adding fuel to fire worsens a situation.

28. The strike was called off at the eleventh hour. This means it was cancelled
A: eleven days early
B: at eleven o'clock exactly
C: long in advance
D: at the last moment
Answer: D
Explanation: Eleventh hour means the very last moment.

29. Mother's word is law in our house. The figure of speech is
A: metaphor
B: simile
C: onomatopoeia
D: alliteration
Answer: A
Explanation: Mother's word is directly called law, a metaphor.

30. The visitor looked every inch a diplomat. This means he looked
A: very short
B: completely like a diplomat
C: unfashionable
D: exactly one inch tall
Answer: B
Explanation: Every inch means in every respect.

31. The pupil gave a cock-and-bull story about his homework. The story was
A: true and verifiable
B: about farm animals
C: nonsense
D: well planned
Answer: C
Explanation: A cock-and-bull story is an absurd fabrication.

32. Rain fell throughout the match, yet the players carried on. The word yet signals
A: addition
B: cause
C: comparison
D: contrast
Answer: D
Explanation: Yet marks contrast between the rain and the players' persistence.

33. Of all the gifts, Ada cherished the least expensive one most. The sentence stresses that value lies in
A: sentiment, not price
B: price alone
C: brand names
D: wrapping
Answer: A
Explanation: Cherishing the cheapest gift most stresses sentiment over cost.

34. The chairman's word was final; none dared question it. The atmosphere described is one of
A: festival
B: authority
C: confusion
D: jubilation
Answer: B
Explanation: A word none dares question conveys firm authority.
