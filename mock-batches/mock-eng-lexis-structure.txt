1. The man was praised for his candour. Candour means
A: sincerity
B: cruelty
C: laziness
D: wealth
Answer: A
Explanation: Candour is openness and sincerity.

2. The plan met with stiff opposition. Stiff here means
A: slight
B: strong
C: friendly
D: muscular
Answer: B
Explanation: Stiff opposition is strong opposition.

3. Choose the word opposite in meaning to abundant.
A: plentiful
B: ample
C: scarce
D: copious
Answer: C
Explanation: The opposite of abundant (plentiful) is scarce.

4. Choose the word opposite in meaning to transparent.
A: clear
B: lucid
C: plain
D: opaque
Answer: D
Explanation: Transparent allows light through; opaque does not.

5. Choose the word nearest in meaning to dilapidated.
A: run-down
B: brand new
C: well painted
D: spacious
Answer: A
Explanation: Dilapidated means falling into disrepair.

6. The chief's generosity is legendary. Legendary means
A: greedy
B: famed
C: recent
D: false
Answer: B
Explanation: Legendary here means widely famed.

7. The committee has been sitting since morning. Sitting here means
A: resting on chairs
B: taking an examination
C: holding a meeting
D: watching a match
Answer: C
Explanation: A committee sits when it holds its meeting.

8. Neither the teacher nor the students ______ present.
A: is
B: was
C: has
D: were
Answer: D
Explanation: With neither...nor, the verb agrees with the nearer subject, students, so were.

9. Each of the boys ______ a book.
A: has
B: have
C: are having
D: were having
Answer: A
Explanation: Each takes a singular verb, has.

10. The two sisters see ______ every weekend.
A: themselves
B: each other
C: one another's
D: each other's
Answer: B
Explanation: Two people see each other.

11. She has been living in Enugu ______ 2019.
A: for
B: from
C: since
D: at
Answer: C
Explanation: Since marks a starting point in time; for marks a length.

12. Hardly had the bell rung ______ the pupils ran out.
A: when
B: then
C: so
D: than
Answer: D
Explanation: The idiom is hardly...when.

13. The house ______ we rented last year has been sold.
A: which
B: who
C: whose
D: whom
Answer: A
Explanation: Which refers to a thing, the house.

14. If I ______ the money, I would buy the textbooks.
A: have
B: had
C: will have
D: am having
Answer: B
Explanation: Second conditional uses the past tense, had.

15. By next December, he ______ the course.
A: will complete
B: completes
C: will have completed
D: completed
Answer: C
Explanation: Future perfect for an action finished by a future point.

16. The luggage ______ heavy, so we took a taxi.
A: were
B: have been
C: are
D: was
Answer: D
Explanation: Luggage is uncountable, so was.

17. He is one of those boys who ______ hard.
A: work
B: works
C: is working
D: has worked
Answer: A
Explanation: The relative who refers to boys, plural, so work.

18. The man was accused ______ theft.
A: for
B: of
C: with
D: on
Answer: B
Explanation: Accused of is the correct collocation.

19. I am not conversant ______ the rules.
A: to
B: on
C: with
D: in
Answer: C
Explanation: Conversant with is the correct collocation.

20. She succeeded ______ passing her examinations.
A: on
B: at
C: for
D: in
Answer: D
Explanation: Succeed in is the correct collocation.

21. The children were delighted ______ the gift.
A: with
B: from
C: at
D: about
Answer: A
Explanation: Delighted with is the standard collocation.

22. The fire broke ______ in the store at midnight.
A: in
B: out
C: up
D: down
Answer: B
Explanation: Break out means to start suddenly.

23. Please look ______ the children while I am away.
A: for
B: up
C: after
D: into
Answer: C
Explanation: Look after means to care for.

24. Our plan fell ______ when the sponsor withdrew.
A: off
B: out
C: down
D: through
Answer: D
Explanation: Fall through means to fail.

25. I ran ______ an old friend at the market.
A: into
B: off
C: away
D: down
Answer: A
Explanation: Run into means to meet by chance.

26. The strike was called ______ after talks with the government.
A: on
B: off
C: up
D: in
Answer: B
Explanation: Call off means to cancel.

27. Put ______ your shoes before entering the mosque.
A: off
B: up
C: on
D: down
Answer: C
Explanation: Put on means to wear.

28. The meeting was put ______ till Friday.
A: on
B: up
C: across
D: off
Answer: D
Explanation: Put off means to postpone.

29. To let the cat out of the bag means to
A: reveal a secret
B: buy a cat
C: free an animal
D: tell a lie
Answer: A
Explanation: The idiom means to reveal a secret.

30. To bury the hatchet means to
A: hide a weapon
B: make peace
C: start a quarrel
D: dig a grave
Answer: B
Explanation: The idiom means to settle a quarrel.

31. To be at daggers drawn means to be
A: fully armed
B: very poor
C: openly hostile
D: on guard duty
Answer: C
Explanation: The idiom means open hostility.

32. To turn a deaf ear means to
A: lose one's hearing
B: listen closely
C: speak loudly
D: refuse to listen
Answer: D
Explanation: The idiom means to ignore what is said.

33. The new policy is a bitter pill to swallow. This means the policy is
A: unpleasant to accept
B: a medicine
C: easy to accept
D: a sweet victory
Answer: A
Explanation: A bitter pill is something hard to accept.

34. He passed his examination with flying colours. This means he passed
A: with difficulty
B: with distinction
C: by cheating
D: at the second attempt
Answer: B
Explanation: With flying colours means with great success.
