1. The three major genres of literature are
A: prose, fiction and essay
B: prose, poetry and drama
C: poetry, novel and novella
D: drama, tragedy and comedy
Answer: B
Explanation: Literature is traditionally divided into three major genres: prose, poetry and drama.
Year: 2014
Difficulty: easy

2. A metaphor differs from a simile because it
A: uses "like" or "as" to make the comparison
B: states the opposite of the truth
C: compares only human beings
D: compares directly without "like" or "as"
Answer: D
Explanation: A simile compares using "like" or "as"; a metaphor makes the comparison directly.
Year: 2012
Difficulty: easy

3. Giving human qualities to non-human things is
A: hyperbole
B: personification
C: alliteration
D: pun
Answer: B
Explanation: Personification attributes human qualities to objects, ideas or animals.
Year: 2018
Difficulty: easy

4. A deliberate exaggeration used for effect, not to be taken literally, is
A: euphemism
B: oxymoron
C: hyperbole
D: irony
Answer: C
Explanation: Hyperbole exaggerates for effect, as in 'I have told you a million times'.
Year: 2011
Difficulty: easy

5. A mild or pleasant expression used in place of a harsh or unpleasant one is
A: sarcasm
B: euphemism
C: metaphor
D: litotes
Answer: B
Explanation: A euphemism softens an unpleasant expression, as 'passed away' stands for 'died'.
Year: 2020
Difficulty: easy

6. Words whose sounds imitate the noises they describe form
A: assonance
B: alliteration
C: rhyme
D: onomatopoeia
Answer: D
Explanation: Onomatopoeia imitates sound, as in 'buzz' and 'hiss'.
Year: 2015
Difficulty: easy

7. An oxymoron is a figure of speech that
A: places two contrasting or contradictory terms side by side
B: repeats two similar sounds
C: joins two unrelated clauses
D: links two complete stanzas
Answer: A
Explanation: An oxymoron pairs contradictory terms, as in 'sweet sorrow' or 'deafening silence'.
Year: 2019
Difficulty: hard

8. Verbal irony occurs when a speaker
A: repeats a phrase for emphasis
B: exaggerates for comic effect
C: says the opposite of what is meant
D: quotes another writer directly
Answer: C
Explanation: In verbal irony the intended meaning is the opposite of the literal words.
Year: 2021
Difficulty: easy

9. Dramatic irony occurs when
A: the audience knows more than the character does
B: the character knows more than the audience
C: two characters exchange witty remarks
D: the play ends in tragedy
Answer: A
Explanation: In dramatic irony the audience holds knowledge the character lacks.
Year: 2013
Difficulty: hard

10. A long narrative poem that celebrates the deeds of a hero is
A: an elegy
B: an epic
C: a ballad
D: a lyric
Answer: B
Explanation: An epic is a long narrative poem on heroic deeds, such as Homer's Odyssey.
Year: 2016
Difficulty: easy

11. An elegy is a poem that
A: praises a ruler at a ceremony
B: tells a comic story in verse
C: laments the dead or reflects on loss
D: describes a landscape in detail
Answer: C
Explanation: An elegy is a meditative poem of mourning, often for the dead.
Year: 2017
Difficulty: easy

12. A soliloquy in drama is
A: a conversation between two characters
B: a character alone on stage speaking thoughts aloud
C: a brief remark to the audience unheard by other characters
D: the closing speech of the play
Answer: B
Explanation: In a soliloquy a character alone reveals thoughts aloud; an aside is the brief audience-directed remark.
Year: 2015
Difficulty: hard

13. The repetition of the same initial consonant sound in nearby words is
A: alliteration
B: assonance
C: refrain
D: onomatopoeia
Answer: A
Explanation: Alliteration repeats initial consonant sounds, as in 'wild and windy'.
Year: 2010
Difficulty: easy

14. Assonance is the repetition of
A: initial consonant sounds
B: vowel sounds within nearby words
C: whole words at the ends of lines
D: grammatical structures
Answer: B
Explanation: Assonance repeats vowel sounds, as in 'the light of the fire'.
Year: 2022
Difficulty: hard

15. The central idea that a literary work explores is its
A: plot

D: mood

Year: 2011
Difficulty: easy
